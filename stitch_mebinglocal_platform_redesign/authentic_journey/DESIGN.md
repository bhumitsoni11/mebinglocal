## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined synthesis of warm editorial travel journalism and effortless consumer utility. Rooted in the spirit of human connection, it strips away the synthetic gloss of corporate SaaS and commercial tourism in favor of an intimate, grounded aesthetic. 

The visual personality balances hospitality, curiosity, and deep trust:
- **Atmosphere:** Earthy, sun-warmed, and culturally grounded without feeling rustic. Clean lines and disciplined negative space provide an uncluttered stage for full-bleed, organic travel photography and authentic personal portraits.
- **Audience:** Discerning travelers seeking meaningful, immersive cultural experiences, paired with verified local residents who act as trusted cultural bridges.
- **Design Movement:** Clean Warm Editorial Minimalism. It features quiet surfaces, high-contrast typography, natural off-white canvases, and deliberate, micro-tactile details that convey immediate credibility and welcome.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in earth tones and natural textures, anchored by a vibrant terracotta primary that communicates warmth, humanity, and vitality.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#E05A47`):** Warm Terracotta. Used for primary calls-to-action, active indicator states, critical interactive highlights, and focal booking buttons. Hover state transitions intentionally to deep terracotta (`#C84937`).
- **Secondary (`#1E3A34`):** Deep Forest Slate. Imparts grounding authority, institutional stability, and mature contrast. Used for key headlines, pill filter chips when active, and high-impact structural framing.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`):** Deep Ochre. An accent color reflecting golden-hour light, artisanal authenticity, and curated regional features.
- **Neutral Core (`#1F2421`):** Deep charcoal olive. Provides rich, high-legibility text without the harsh sterility of pure black (`#000000`).

### Surface & Background Tokens
- **Background Canvas (`#FBFBF9`):** A linen-inspired, warm off-white canvas that reduces eye strain and provides organic depth compared to clinical digital whites.
- **Surface / Card (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure white applied to interactive cards, popovers, and elevated modal panels.
- **Surface Subdued (`#F4F3EF`):** Muted stone gray used for recessed blocks, secondary badges, input containers, and structural card footers.
- **Border / Divider (`#E5E5DF`):** Low-contrast architectural line work that delineates content without creating visual noise.

### Functional & Signal Accents
- **Verified Trust (`#059669` / `#10B981`):** Subdued botanical emerald for verified local friend indicators, identity confirmations, and safety signals.
- **Rating Gold (`#F59E0B`):** Warm amber used exclusively for rating stars and review scores.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a dual-sans system:
- **Headings & Display (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its geometric structure softened by contemporary humanist contours. Tight tracking (-0.015em to -0.025em) and crisp line-heights lend an authoritative, high-end editorial presence.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Inter`):** Selected for neutral, utilitarian optical performance at small-to-mid sizes. Generous line-heights (1.5 to 1.6x) ensure effortless reading through detailed cultural itineraries and host bios.
- **Micro-Copy (`label-caps`):** Used strictly for metadata tags, status categories, and overlines, rendered with wide letter-spacing and medium/bold weights to maintain legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a responsive fluid grid that prioritizes structural rhythm, consistent breathing room, and clear focal planes.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid, max content container of `1280px`, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `3rem` (48px) margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column fluid grid with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins.

### Spacing Philosophy
Spacing tokens are applied mathematically to maintain clean vertical and horizontal cadence:
- `space-xs` (4px) & `space-sm` (8px): Icon-to-text spacing, rating stars, inline pill padding, and tight micro-groups.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard inner card padding, form field gaps, and compact list gaps.
- `space-lg` (24px): Primary card padding, separation between distinct component blocks, and responsive row gutters.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section breaks, headline-to-content buffers, and major narrative shifts.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through tonal stratification supported by soft, warm-tinted ambient diffusion. Heavy artificial drop shadows are strictly avoided to keep surfaces natural and light.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background canvas (`#FBFBF9`) with zero elevation. Recessed blocks and inputs rest at this tier or use `#F4F3EF`.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Tiles):** Surface pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a subtle architectural outline (`1px solid #E5E5DF`). At rests, cards project a soft, warm ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Dropdowns):** Interactive elements rise on hover to `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.07), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Search Capsule & Navigation):** Floating navigation and multi-segment pill controls float prominently over imagery and canvas: `box-shadow: 0 16px 32px -8px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.09), 0 6px 16px -4px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Sheets):** Backdrop blur of `8px` combined with a semi-opaque neutral scrim (`rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.45)`). The modal surface features `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The shape system utilizes deliberate curvature variations to establish a tactile hierarchy:
- **Base Cards & Media Containers:** Use `rounded-lg` (0.75rem / 12px) to softly frame rich visual content without looking childlike or overly bubbled.
- **Buttons, Modals, & Inputs:** Use standard `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px) for an intentional, crisp, and tactile interaction profile.
- **Interactive Badges, Search Bars, & Filters:** Use fully circular profiles (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to indicate continuous touch-readiness and pill containment.
- **Photography Rule:** All photography must feature corner radii consistent with its parent card (`12px`) or a minimum of `8px` when isolated, avoiding razor-sharp edges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Warm Terracotta background (`#E05A47`), pure white text, `8px` border radius, horizontal padding of `24px`, vertical padding of `12px`. Transitions on hover to Deep Terracotta (`#C84937`). No borders. Active state compresses scale down by 1% (`scale-98`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Pure white or transparent background, `1px solid #E5E5DF`, Neutral Dark text (`#1F2421`). Hover transitions to `#F4F3EF` background and `#1F2421` border.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** No background, no border. Neutral Dark text (`#1F2421`) with underline appearing on hover. Used for inline narrative actions and dismissals.

### Floating Multi-Segment Search Capsule
- **Container:** Fully rounded (`9999px`), pure white surface, Level 3 floating shadow, hairline border (`#E5E5DF`).
- **Segments:** Split into four discrete touch zones (Where, When, Experience Type, Who). Segments are separated by vertical hairline dividers (`#E5E5DF`, `24px` height).
- **Segment States:** Hovering illuminates a segment with `#F4F3EF` fill (`rounded-full`). Focused segment activates a clean inset border. Contains an embedded circular Primary search button (`44px x 44px`) anchored on the right edge.

### Cards (Host & Experience)
- **Structure:** Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`), `12px` border radius, `1px solid #E5E5DF`, hidden overflow.
- **Media Presentation:** `4:3` or `1:1` aspect ratio image container. Images must use warm natural lighting and full bleed inside the container.
- **Badging:** Top-left floats the "Verified Local" pill. Top-right accommodates a heart/save icon with an ambient frosted glass circle backdrop (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).
- **Body:** `16px` padding beneath the image. Primary title in `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`headline-sm`), localized pricing with bold per-hour/per-experience notation, and rating stars rendered in `#F59E0B`.

### Verified Local Badge
- **Container:** High-density pill (`rounded-full`), padded `4px 10px`.
- **Styling:** Soft botanical emerald tint background (`rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.1)`), hairline border (`rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.2)`), and emerald text (`#059669`) in `label-caps`. 
- **Icon:** Preceded by a `12px` verified checkmark or heraldic seal icon.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Container:** `8px` border radius, `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E5E5DF`. Text styled with `body-md` in `#1F2421`.
- **Padding:** `12px 16px` internal padding.
- **Focus:** Sharp transition to `1.5px solid #1E3A34` without aggressive outer glow rings. Error states use `#E05A47` border with companion inline micro-copy.

### Filter Chips
- **Resting:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E5E5DF`, `rounded-full`, padding `8px 18px`, `label-md` typography.
- **Active:** Deep Forest Slate background (`#1E3A34`), pure white text, border colored `#1E3A34`.
