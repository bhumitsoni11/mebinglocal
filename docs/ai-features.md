# AI Features

## 1. AI Travel Assistant
Answers travel-related queries using personalized traveler context.

## 2. AI Itinerary Generator
Generates structured multi-day itineraries based on budget, style, and interests.

## 3. AI Local Friend Matching
Combines Prisma database filtering with AI semantic ranking to suggest the best matches.

## 4. AI Recommendations
Hybrid rule-based (DB candidates) + AI ranking engine.

## 5. AI Review Summarization
Aggregates recent reviews into pros/cons. Caches result in Redis.

## 6. AI Translation
Preserves travel tone and entity names across languages.

## 7. AI Safety Intelligence
Analyzes platform trust signals without discrimination.

## 8. AI Discovery
Extracts structured intent from natural language search queries.
